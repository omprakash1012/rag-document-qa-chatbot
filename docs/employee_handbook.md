Employee Handbook

Welcome to the company. This handbook summarizes the core policies every employee should know.

Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9 AM to 5 PM, Monday through Friday, in the employee's local time zone. Employees on hybrid schedules are expected to be in the office at least two days per week, coordinated with their manager.

Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, plus 10 company holidays. PTO accrues monthly and unused days roll over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following year. PTO requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for planned leave.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements are evaluated case by case by HR and the employee's department head.

Health Benefits

The company offers medical, dental, and vision coverage starting on the first day of employment. Employees can enroll dependents during open enrollment each November or within 30 days of a qualifying life event.

Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date, with an itemized receipt attached. Reimbursements are processed within two pay cycles.

Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated and should be reported to HR immediately.

Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December. Reviews include a self-assessment, manager feedback, and goal-setting for the next cycle.
