Product FAQ

What is the product?

The product is a cloud-based analytics platform that helps teams track key metrics, build dashboards, and share reports across the organization without writing SQL.

What pricing plans are available?

There are three plans: Starter (up to 5 users, core dashboards), Team (up to 25 users, scheduled reports and alerts), and Enterprise (unlimited users, SSO, dedicated support, and custom data connectors). Pricing is billed monthly or annually, with a discount for annual billing.

How does data stay secure?

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256. Enterprise plans support single sign-on (SSO) via SAML and role-based access controls to restrict who can view or edit specific dashboards.

Can I connect my own data sources?

Yes. The platform supports direct connectors for common databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake) and warehouses, as well as CSV upload and a REST API for custom integrations.

What is the uptime guarantee?

The Enterprise plan includes a 99.9% uptime SLA. Status and incident history are published on the public status page.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from Account Settings under Billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle, and your data remains exportable for 30 days afterward.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, all new accounts get a 14-day free trial of the Team plan with no credit card required. After the trial, you can choose a paid plan or downgrade to a limited free tier.

Who do I contact for support?

Starter and Team customers can reach support via email with a typical response time of one business day. Enterprise customers get a dedicated support channel with guaranteed 4-hour response times during business hours.
